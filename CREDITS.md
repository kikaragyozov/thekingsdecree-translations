## Initial translations

The initial v2.0.0 translations for all 14 languages were AI-assisted, with every mechanical term cross-referenced and locked to the official MegaCrit vanilla translations for each language. The `THEKINGSDECREE-generic.selectionScreenPrompt` value on every language was copied verbatim from MegaCrit's own `BRAIN_LEECH.pages.SHARE_KNOWLEDGE.selectionScreenPrompt` — so that specific string is 100% vanilla quality by construction.

Where AI-assisted prose falls short of native-speaker fluency, this repository exists to close the gap.
